# Installation Guide: Minecraft Chinese Pinyin Resource Pack

Follow these instructions to install the Minecraft Chinese Pinyin Resource Pack on **Java Edition** or **Bedrock Edition**.

---

## Java Edition

### Step 1: Download the Resource Pack
Download your desired release variant:
* `Minecraft-Chinese-Pinyin-v1.2.0.zip` (Standard tone marks)
* `Minecraft-Chinese-Pinyin-v1.2.0-Numbers.zip` (Tone numbers)
* `Minecraft-Chinese-Pinyin-v1.2.0-Recall.zip` (Recall mode)

Do not unzip the archive unless your launcher requires unpacked folders; Minecraft natively loads `.zip` packs.

### Step 2: Locate your resourcepacks Folder
1. Launch **Minecraft: Java Edition**.
2. From the main menu, go to **Options...** -> **Resource Packs...**.
3. Click the **Open Pack Folder** button at the bottom left.
4. Or manually open:
   * **Windows**: `%appdata%\.minecraft\resourcepacks`
   * **macOS**: `~/Library/Application Support/minecraft/resourcepacks`
   * **Linux**: `~/.minecraft/resourcepacks`

### Step 3: Activate the Pack
1. Move or copy the `.zip` file into the `resourcepacks` directory.
2. Return to Minecraft, click the arrow to move the pack to **Selected**, and click **Done**.

### Step 4: Language Selection
Under **Options** -> **Language...**, select:
* **中文 (拼音)** - Mandarin with Pinyin
* **中文 (注音)** - Taiwan Traditional Chinese with Zhuyin (Bopomofo)
* **廣東話 (粵拼)** - Hong Kong Cantonese with Jyutping
* Or standard dialects (`zh_cn`, `zh_tw`, `zh_hk`, `lzh`) or **English (US)**.

---

## Bedrock Edition

1. Download `Minecraft-Chinese-Pinyin-v1.2.0.mcpack`.
2. Double-click or open the `.mcpack` file on your device (Windows, Android, iOS, iPadOS).
3. Minecraft Bedrock Edition will launch and import the pack automatically.
4. Navigate to **Settings** -> **Global Resources** (or individual world settings), select **Minecraft Chinese with Pinyin**, and click **Activate**.
